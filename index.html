We are giving best services to our clients since very long time

We are a specialized manufacturer of HVAC Prefabricated Ducts designed for industrial, commercial & residential applications. Our facility is equipped with advanced sheet metal forming machinery to produce high-precision ducts with consistent quality and faster production cycles.

Our manufacturing plant utilizes state-of-the-art automated CNC technology to deliver high-performance, leak-tested, and ready-to-install ducting. By moving fabrication from the job site to our controlled facility, we ensure superior quality, reduced waste, and significantly faster project timelines.

At REFROTECH, we believe that the highest-quality ducting isn’t just manufactured; it’s crafted. While our facility utilizes the latest CNC and automated technologies, the true core of our plant is our people.
